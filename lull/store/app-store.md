## Description (max 4000)
```
Every other breathing app hands you a pattern: four in, seven hold, eight out.

But if your body is at eighteen breaths a minute and an app tells you to breathe at
six, you do not relax. You fail at breathing — and then you feel worse about it.

Lull starts where you actually are.

IT MEASURES YOU FIRST
Breathe normally and tap once on each out-breath. Thirty seconds, six taps. Lull
learns your real rate right now — not your average, not a guess.

THEN IT LEADS YOU DOWN
The ring begins at your rate and slows. Not in steps you can feel, but along a curve.
Target: six breaths a minute.

YOUR OUT-BREATH STRETCHES
Slowing is not the lever. A longer exhale is. Across the session the out-breath grows
to about 1.6 times the in-breath.

THE SCREEN FADES
By the last third it is nearly dark. Nothing invites your eyes back open.

• No account, no sign-up, no email
• No internet connection required
• Nothing leaves your phone — there is no server to send it to
• No ads at all — not during a session, not after
• 5, 9 or 15 minutes

IMPORTANT
Lull is a relaxation tool, not a medical device. It does not diagnose or treat any
condition. If you have a breathing, heart or neurological condition, are pregnant, or
take medication affecting breathing or heart rate, talk to your doctor first. Stop if
you feel dizzy. Never use while driving.
```
