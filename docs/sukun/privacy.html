Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026 · Sükûn (iOS and Android)

This policy explains what happens to your data when you use Sükûn. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

1. The short version

No account, no server. We do not ask for your name, e-mail or phone number. Your location, settings, worship records, notes and dhikr counts never leave your phone — they do not reach us, because we run no server to receive them. Delete the app and all of it goes with it.

2. Who we are

The app is published by the developer named on the store listing. Contact: kusgrupgames@gmail.com. For privacy questions, write with "Privacy" in the subject.

3. Location

Prayer times and the qibla direction depend on where you are, so the app needs a latitude and a longitude. There are two ways to give it:

  • Grant location permission and the app takes a single reading. There is no continuous tracking; it will not read again until you change city.
  • Decline and pick your city from a list. The app works in full either way.

In both cases the coordinates are used for calculation on your phone and stay there. They are not sent anywhere.

4. What you record

Your worship log, missed-prayer counters, dhikr sessions, bookmarks, verse notes, favourites and settings are kept in your phone's own storage. We cannot see, read or back them up. If your phone backup is enabled they may be included in Apple's or Google's backup — that backup belongs to you, not to us.

5. The only times the app goes online

The app reaches the internet for three things. None of them carries your identity:

  • Recitation playback streams audio from the Islamic Network CDN. The request says which verse you are listening to, not who you are.
  • Content updates read a text file hosted on GitHub Pages. It is the same file everyone downloads.
  • Ads, if shown, involve Google's advertising SDK (see section 6).

Prayer times, qibla, the Qur'an text, the translation, dhikr and every calculation work offline.

6. Advertising

The free tier uses Google's advertising SDK, which may use your device's advertising identifier to serve ads; what it collects is set out in Google's own policy. The limits we impose are enforced in the app's code:

  • Content rating is pinned to G.
  • Alcohol, gambling, dating, politics, religion, sexual content, weapons, tobacco, drugs, cosmetic surgery and astrology categories are blocked.
  • No ads in the 15 minutes before a prayer time and the 30 minutes after it.
  • No ads at all on the Qur'an reader, qibla, dhikr counter and prayer guide screens.

A Pro subscription turns ads off entirely.

7. Analytics

Analytics is off by default, and even when enabled it never sends location, worship records, notes or search terms. Those fields are masked at the code level.

8. Children

The app is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect data from children. It does not collect personal data from anyone.

9. Your rights

For GDPR and KVKK rights (access, rectification, erasure, portability) there is technically nothing to request: your data is only ever on your device. Deleting the app deletes everything it holds. You are still welcome to write to the address above with any question.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated. Any change that materially affects how data is handled will also be announced inside the app.
