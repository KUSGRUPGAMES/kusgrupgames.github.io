Terms of Use

Last updated: 20 September 2026 · BEŞ (iOS and Android)

1. What the app is

BEŞ is a tool. It computes prayer times from the sun's position, finds the qibla by great-circle bearing, and displays the Qur'an with a Turkish translation. It does not issue religious rulings and does not stand in for a scholar. For questions about your own situation, ask someone qualified to answer them.

2. How accurate the calculations are

Prayer times depend on the calculation method, your coordinates, elevation and any per-prayer minute adjustment you set; they can differ by a minute or two from an official local timetable. The Hijri date is arithmetic and can fall a day either side of a sighting-based announcement. The qibla arrow is only as good as your phone's magnetometer. Every one of these caveats is also stated on the screen it applies to — we would rather tell you than have you find out.

3. Content and its sources

The Arabic text of the Qur'an is taken verbatim from the Tanzil Project and is not altered. The Turkish translation is Elmalılı Hamdi Yazır's, which is in the public domain. Supplications and reference articles were written for this app; they are not presented as verses or hadith, and are not quoted as such. Recitations stream from the Islamic Network CDN and remain the copyright of the reciters.

If you spot an error in any of it, tell us and it gets corrected. If a rights holder objects to something, it comes out.

4. Subscription

Pro is sold monthly or yearly through the app store you installed from. Price, period and renewal terms are shown on the purchase screen and in the store record. A subscription renews at the end of each period unless you cancel, and cancelling is done in your store settings, not here. Refunds follow the store's own rules.

The free tier is not a demo: prayer times, qibla, the full Qur'an, the translation, the dhikr counter, missed-prayer tracking, supplications and the guides all work without paying.
