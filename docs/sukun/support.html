Common questions

The times are a minute or two off from my mosque.

Different calculation methods and different Asr measures give different answers — that is expected, not a fault. Go to Profile → Prayer settings, pick the method your local timetable uses, and set a per-prayer minute adjustment if you still want them to line up exactly. For Turkey, use Diyanet.

Notifications don't arrive.

Check three things: the phone's notification permission, the prayer notifications switch inside the app, and whether battery optimisation is restricting the app. Phones only hold a limited number of scheduled notifications at once, so the app re-arms them further ahead every time you open it — opening it now and then is enough.

The qibla arrow keeps moving.

Compasses are thrown off by magnetic fields. Move away from metal desks, speakers, cars and electronics, then move the phone in a figure of eight to recalibrate. The app tells you when accuracy drops instead of pretending the reading is good.

The recitation won't play.

Streaming needs a connection. Surahs you download play offline: Qur'an → Recitation → the download button next to the surah. Some reciters exist at only one bitrate; the app falls back to whatever is available.

The Hijri date is a day out.

It is computed arithmetically and can differ by a day from a sighting-based announcement. You can correct it by up to ±2 days on the Hijri calendar screen.

How do I back up my records?

They live on your phone and we hold no copy. If your phone's own backup (iCloud or Google) is on, the app's data is part of it. There is no cross-device sync yet.

How do I cancel the subscription?

Subscriptions are managed by the store, not by us. On iPhone: Settings → your Apple Account → Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store → Payments and subscriptions. Pro stays on until the end of the period you already paid for.

Reporting a problem

These four things make a bug report easy to act on: phone model, app version (Profile → About), the city and calculation method you have selected, and what you were doing when it happened.
