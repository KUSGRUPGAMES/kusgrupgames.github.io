Apps

Small, offline, no account. Three one-touch games and two tools.

Slot: Fit the Shape

Walls fall towards you and every wall has a hole cut in exactly your shape — turned to a different angle. One tap turns you 90°. Finish the turn before contact for a CLEAN fit.

Latch: One Tap Swing

Hold and a rope shoots to the nearest hook. You swing. Let go inside the gold window at 45° and the throw counts PERFECT. Miss the next hook and the drop below ends the run.

Orbita: One Tap Orbit Jump

Your ball circles a planet. Tap and it flies off along the tangent of its spin — not towards where you are looking. Land on the next orbit, dodge the red arcs, and see how far you get.

Privacy · Terms · Support · Türkçe

Lull — Breathe Yourself Down

Other breathing apps hand you a fixed pattern. If your body is at eighteen breaths a minute and an app says breathe at six, you don't relax — you fail at breathing. Lull measures your actual rate first, then leads you down from there, stretching the out-breath as it goes. The screen fades to dark as you sink.

Seher — Prayer Times, Qibla, Tasbih

Prayer times computed on the phone from the sun's position — no connection needed, anywhere on earth. Countdown to the next prayer, per-prayer notifications, a qibla compass with the distance to the Kaaba, a thirty-day table for the whole month, a tasbih counter, and the ninety-nine names. Also 158 ready greeting cards for the holy nights and both Eids, and a step-by-step course in the Qur'anic alphabet. Seven calculation methods and a per-prayer minute adjustment, so it can be lined up exactly with your local calendar.

BEŞ — Prayer Times and the Qur'an

Everything Seher does, plus the Qur'an itself: all 6,236 verses with Elmalılı Hamdi Yazır's Turkish translation, eighteen reciters you can stream or download for offline listening, bookmarks and verse notes, and search that works in both the Arabic and the translation. Prayer times, qibla, dhikr and every calculation still run on the phone with no connection. No account, no server — what you record stays on your device.
