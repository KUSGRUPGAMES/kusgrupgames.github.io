## Description (4000)

```
BEŞ brings prayer times, the qibla direction and the complete Holy Quran together in one calm app. Prayer times are calculated on your phone from the position of the sun — no server, no internet needed. It works on a plane, abroad, anywhere without signal.

YOUR WORSHIP RECORDS STAY WITH YOU
Prayer times, qibla, the Quran, dhikr, missed-prayer (qada) tracking and the worship log need no account. Everything works without signing in, and your records stay on your phone. Sign in with Google or Apple and they sync with your account, so they follow you to a new phone. Your location is never sent.

OPTIONAL COMMUNITY
Ask for prayers on the prayer board and pray for others, talk in topic-based chat rooms, and share the 30 juz of a khatm with friends. Sign in with Google or Apple to join; other people only see the nickname you choose. You can delete your account from inside the app at any time.

COUNTDOWN TO THE NEXT PRAYER
The next prayer's name, time and remaining time, second by second. All five prayers and sunrise at a glance, and a monthly timetable on one screen.

SEVEN CALCULATION METHODS
Diyanet (Turkey), Muslim World League, ISNA, Egypt, Umm al-Qura, Karachi and Tehran, with separate Hanafi and Shafi'i Asr. Adjust each prayer by minutes to match your local mosque exactly.

THE HOLY QURAN — ALL 6,236 VERSES
The Arabic text is taken verbatim from the Tanzil Project. The Turkish translation by Elmalılı Hamdi Yazır (public domain) is included and works offline. Read Arabic only, Arabic with translation, or translation only.

EIGHTEEN RECITERS
Abdul Basit, Mishary Rashid Alafasy, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, Abdul Rahman Al-Sudais and fourteen more. Stream, or download surahs to listen offline.

SEARCH
Search the Arabic text and the translation together. Type without diacritics; references like "2:255" work too.

QIBLA COMPASS
Direction and distance to the Kaaba. When the compass accuracy drops, the app tells you instead of pointing you the wrong way.

DHIKR COUNTER
Eight ready-made dhikr, targets, your own dhikr, and haptic feedback on every tap. Daily, weekly and monthly statistics and streaks.

WORSHIP TRACKING
Qada prayer counters, a day-by-day worship log, fasting tracker, khatm and muqabala planning — all on your phone.

RAMADAN
Suhoor and iftar countdown, a month-long imsakiye, fasting log.

ALSO
Zakat calculator (choose the nisab standard; the method is shown on screen), the 99 Names of Allah, 98 supplications including 64 from the Quran, a prayer guide, Hijri calendar and upcoming religious days, a Hajj and Umrah guide, and a 43-article Islamic knowledge library.

FIVE LANGUAGES
The whole interface is available in Turkish, English, Arabic, German and French; Arabic switches the layout to right-to-left. The Quran translation (Elmalılı) and long-form articles are in Turkish.

WORSHIP IS ALWAYS FREE
Prayer times, qibla, the Quran and translation, supplications, dhikr and qada tracking are always free. The free version shows ads — but never in the Quran reader, and never just before or right after a prayer time.

BEŞ PRO
Monthly, yearly or lifetime. Removes all ads and unlocks every lesson of the Learn course plus community extras (writing in chat rooms, creating khatm groups, 5 prayer requests a day). Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period; manage or cancel them in your App Store account settings.

AN HONEST NOTE
Prayer times depend on the method, location and elevation and may differ from official announcements by a minute or two. The Hijri date is arithmetic and may be a day off from a sighting-based announcement. We say so on the relevant screens. BEŞ is a helper tool; it does not issue religious rulings.

Privacy Policy: https://kusgrupgames.github.io/bes/privacy.html
```
